Oldest restaurants in Tampa

Open at the same address since 1960 or earlier, as each place or a published history documents it, oldest first. The note under each says what the year rests on. Statewide: Florida's oldest restaurants.

  1. Columbia Restaurant

    2117 E 7th Ave, Tampa

    Cuban & cafecito On the menu: Cuban sandwich (house-baked ham, mojo pork, Genoa salami, Swiss, pickles, mustard on La Segunda bread), Spanish-Cuban plates, flamenco dinner show · Since 1905

    Block-long Spanish-Cuban dining hall in Ybor City, run by the founding family and known for its tiled rooms, flamenco show and Tampa-style Cuban.

    The restaurant says Casimiro Hernandez Sr. founded it in 1905 in a 1903 corner saloon that is part of today's block-long Ybor building; it calls itself "Florida's Oldest Restaurant℠" (its service mark, an attributed claim).

  2. Bern's Steak House

    1208 S Howard Ave, Tampa

    MICHELIN Recommended On the menu: dry-aged steaks, dessert room, wine list · Since 1956

    South Tampa steakhouse famous for its huge wine cellar and upstairs Harry Waugh Dessert Room, with wine lists dated 2026.

    Bern's says it was founded in 1956 and created from a business block on Howard Avenue that held a grocery, barber shop and dime store; it marks its 70th anniversary in 2026.

    bernssteakhouse.com
