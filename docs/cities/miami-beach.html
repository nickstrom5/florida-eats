  1. Joe's Stone Crab

    11 Washington Ave, Miami Beach

    MICHELIN RecommendedAmerica's ClassicStone crabKeys classic On the menu: stone crab claws, mustard sauce, hash browns, creamed spinach, fried chicken, Key lime pie · Since 1918 · Season: Seasonally closed Sept 14 – Oct 16, 2026 (reopens Oct 16 for its 114th season); stone crab claws Oct 15 – May 1 (FWC season).

    South Pointe institution where stone crabs were first served chilled and cracked in the 1920s, run by the Weiss family's fourth generation; Joe's Take Away next door sells claws to go.

    Joe Weiss opened a lunch counter at Smith's bathing casino in 1913; in 1918 he and Jennie bought a bungalow on Biscayne Street and opened Joe's Restaurant, and the restaurant's history says it still operates in the family's house. The tourism bureau calls it Miami Beach's oldest restaurant.

    joesstonecrab.com
