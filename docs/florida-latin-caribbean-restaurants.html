Florida Latin & Caribbean restaurants

40 restaurants, bakeries and cafés across Florida for the Latin American and Caribbean cooking beyond Cuban, each checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source. Towns with the most: Miami (10), Kissimmee (8), St. Augustine (3), Jacksonville (3), Tampa (3).

Beyond the Cuban sandwich

Florida eats the whole region: Haitian griot and pikliz, Venezuelan arepas and cachapas, Colombian bandeja paisa, Puerto Rican mofongo and pernil, Peruvian ceviche, Nicaraguan fritanga and Dominican mangú. The dish line under each place says what it serves, from its own menu or a recent source.

What's on the list

The dishes we found most often: arepas (5), tostones (4), mofongo (4), puerto rican bakery goods (3), pabellón (3), pernil (3).

How the list was built: each place was checked against a 2025 or 2026 source, such as its own website or menu, recent local news or an official tourism listing, with the address checked. Places with no current source or a dead website were left out. Nothing here comes from review sites, and the order is by region and town, not by anyone's rating. Menus change, so check before you go.

In the Florida Eats app, the same list sorts by distance from you, and each place opens Apple Maps' own card for live hours, photos and directions.
