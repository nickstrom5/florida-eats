The sandwich and the coffee window

A Cuban sandwich is roast pork, ham, Swiss cheese, pickles and mustard on Cuban bread, pressed flat and hot. Tampa's version, from the Ybor City cigar-factory days, usually adds Genoa salami. The coffee comes strong and sweet: a cafecito is a single shot of Cuban espresso, and a colada is a bigger cup poured into thimble-sized cups to share, often from a walk-up window, the ventanita.
