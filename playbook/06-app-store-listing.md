## Description
The first three lines show before "more", so they carry the pitch.

```
Florida's restaurants, and the Cuban sandwiches, stone crabs and oyster bars worth the drive.

Florida Eats is a free guide to eating in Florida: 467 places we checked by hand (Cuban sandwiches and coffee windows, stone crabs, grouper and seafood shacks, Keys classics, oyster bars, fish camps, Latin and Caribbean kitchens and the state's oldest restaurants), MICHELIN and James Beard honorees, and 52,790 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries in 581 towns. No account, no ads.

FLORIDA GUIDES
• Cuban & Cafecito: Cuban sandwiches, bakeries and ventanitas, from Calle Ocho to Ybor City.
• Stone Crabs: in season Oct 15 – May 1.
• Grouper & Seafood Shacks: grouper sandwiches on the Gulf and Atlantic.
• Keys Classics: key lime pie, conch fritters and Keys seafood.
• Oyster Bars and Fish Camps & Smoked Fish: raw bars, Old Florida fish camps and smoked fish dip.
• Latin & Caribbean: Haitian, Venezuelan, Colombian, Puerto Rican and more.
• MICHELIN & James Beard: the MICHELIN Guide Florida 2026 selection and James Beard honorees, as facts.
• Oldest Places: founding years checked at the same address, oldest first.

THE STATE'S OWN INSPECTIONS
Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants inspects every licensed restaurant. Each place shows the state's result at its latest visit, in the state's own words, with the date and its violation counts. Florida doesn't grade restaurants, and neither does this app.

FIND IT FAST
• Sort by distance, or browse the map.
• Search by name, town, street or dish: "cuban sandwich ybor", "stone crab", "southwest 8th street".
• Filter by town or cuisine, hide chains.
• Save places for your next trip.
• Find hand-checked and honored places from iPhone search (Spotlight).

LIVE DETAILS FROM APPLE MAPS
Tap "Ratings, hours & photos" on any place to open Apple Maps' own place card, with current hours, photos, ratings and directions.

HOW THE LISTS ARE BUILT
Every place in the Florida guides was checked by hand in fall 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: the place's own website or menu, local news or an official tourism listing. Restaurants come from the State of Florida's food service licenses (DBPR) and Overture Maps open data. Website links are checked too: each must be the restaurant's own page. Nothing is ranked by star ratings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location, if you share it, only sorts lists on your device. Saved places stay on your device.

Places open and close, and some close for part of the summer or after a storm, so check before you go. Spot a mistake? Email work-with-nick@gmail.com.

An independent app, not affiliated with any restaurant, team, chain or government agency, the MICHELIN Guide or the James Beard Foundation. MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Place data from Florida DBPR public records and Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0) and others; sources and licenses are on the About tab.
```
