# fl-eats

Three things built from one pipeline (modeled on `../wi-eats/` and `../co-eats/`, which are never modified):

1. **The web leaderboard**: every Florida restaurant on several boards, published as a private claude.ai Artifact from `site/` (generated by
   `scripts/make-leaderboard.py`). It uses the UCSD Google Local 2021 snapshot for ratings, labeled "2021" everywhere, so `site/` never goes
   in a public repo.
2. **Florida Eats**, a free iPhone and iPad app (`FloridaEats/`, XcodeGen `project.yml`). No Google-derived data: places come from the
   State of Florida's food service licenses and Overture Maps open data. Ratings and hours come live from Apple Maps' own place card.
3. **The website** in `docs/` (generated by `scripts/make-site.py`): landing page, guides, county and city pages, the web app at
   `/explore/`, privacy, terms. QA with `scripts/qa-site.py`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and commands. `playbook/` holds:
- `12-sources.md`: sources and calibration
- `06-app-store-listing.md`: the App Store listing
- `13-app-review-reply.md`: the App Review reply and the recording script
- `14-site.md`: the website
- `15-launch-steps.md`: every outward step, none done yet
- `hub/`: the eatsranked.com hub entry

## Official records
- **Florida DBPR, Division of Hotels and Restaurants** (public records, Ch. 119 F.S., published weekly). It covers food service licenses
  (every seating, non-seating, catering and theme park license), routine and complaint inspections since July 2023, emergency closure
  reports and final orders. The app and site show DBPR's own disposition and result group (Met Inspection Standards / Follow-Up Inspection
  Required / Facility Temporarily Closed) with dates and violation counts. Florida doesn't grade restaurants, and neither do we.
- DBPR's and FDACS's inspection searches are one-search-at-a-time forms; they're linked, never scraped.

## Hand-checked research
`data/research/` (private) holds:
- the MICHELIN Guide Florida 2026 selection (200 restaurants) and James Beard honors (finalists never called wins)
- regional lists of Cuban sandwiches and cafecito windows, stone crabs, grouper and seafood shacks, Keys classics, oyster bars, fish camps,
  Latin and Caribbean kitchens and the oldest places, each with a 2025–26 source
- closures with sources
- leads that couldn't be verified (`data/research/app/LEADS_*.md`)
